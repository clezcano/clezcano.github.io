Research Statement

I am a researcher and engineer situated at the intersection of Discrete Optimization and Generative AI. My research agenda focuses on the "Validity Crisis" in generative models for scientific discovery: developing methods that enforce hard physical constraints (Symmetries/Wyckoff Positions) within probabilistic latent spaces.

My background converges two distinct fields: Optimization in Constrained Spaces and Statistical Generative Modeling. I am currently leveraging this dual expertise to build Physics-Aware Generative Models for Crystallography.
