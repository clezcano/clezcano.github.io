Research Focus

I am a researcher and engineer specializing in Data Mining, Synthetic Dataset Generation, and Optimization Algorithms.

My current work focuses on generative models for complex data structures. I am currently developing new approaches for high-fidelity synthetic data generation, targeting submission to top-tier conferences such as NeurIPS.
