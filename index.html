Current Research Agenda

I am working at the intersection of Geometric Deep Learning and Material Science. My research targets the "Validity Crisis" in generative models: how to generate novel crystal structures that strictly obey physical symmetries (Space Groups) rather than just minimizing reconstruction loss.

Currently, I am developing Differentiable Symmetry Projection (DSP), a framework to enforce Wyckoff position constraints within continuous latent spaces. This work converges my background in Discrete Optimization (Ant Colony/Hard Constraints) with Generative Modeling (Synthetic Data/Probabilistic Models).

Target: High-fidelity generation of stable crystals. Preparing submission for NeurIPS 2026.

Research Foundation (The "Zig-Zag" Convergence)

My path to Geometric AI is built on two foundational pillars:

  • Pillar 1: Structure-Preserving Generative Models. Developed methods to generate synthetic transactional datasets that preserve statistical validity. This solved the "privacy vs. utility" trade-off in database mining.
  • Pillar 2: Optimization in Constrained Spaces. Mastered the application of meta-heuristics (Ant Colony Optimization) to 3D packing problems in Bioinformatics. This provided the mathematical intuition for handling 3D Euclidean constraints in my current work.
